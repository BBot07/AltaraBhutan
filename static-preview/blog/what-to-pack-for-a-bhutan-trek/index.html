What To Pack For A Bhutan Trek

25 July 2026

Bhutan's treks climb quickly, from warm valleys to cold passes above 4,000 meters, and the weather can shift in an afternoon. The good news is that you do not need a great deal of gear to be comfortable: a small, well-chosen kit and a layered approach are enough. Here is the list our guides give guests before every trek.

  1. 01

    Footwear And Feet

    • Broken-in, waterproof hiking boots with ankle support and a firm sole
    • Wool or synthetic socks, at least three pairs, plus a lighter pair for camp
    • Camp shoes or sandals for the evenings
    • Gaiters, useful in wet or snowy conditions on higher passes
  2. 02

    Clothing In Layers

    • Moisture-wicking base layers for the day, and a warm thermal set for sleeping
    • A fleece or light insulated jacket, and a warm down jacket for camp
    • Waterproof jacket and trousers that you are happy to wear all day if it rains
    • Trekking trousers that dry quickly, and a hat, gloves and buff for the passes
  3. 03

    Sleeping And Carrying

    Our crew provides tents, mattresses and a camp kitchen, and your main bag travels on pack animals. Bring a good sleeping bag, comfortable to roughly −10°C for higher routes (we can arrange rentals), and a daypack of around 25 to 30 liters for what you need during the walking day. Keep your main duffel soft-sided and packed in dry bags, as animals carry limited weight.

  4. 04

    Sun, Water And Health

    • Sunglasses, a wide-brimmed hat, and strong sunscreen and lip balm for high altitude
    • A refillable water bottle or bladder; your crew provides safe boiled water
    • Trekking poles, which protect the knees on long descents
    • A personal first-aid kit with blister care, and any medication you take regularly
    • Speak to your doctor about altitude before you travel, especially for routes above 4,000 meters
  5. 05

    Small Things That Matter

    A headlamp with spare batteries, a power bank, a light towel, wet wipes, a small personal toilet kit for the trail, and a notebook. Leave non-essentials in Thimphu: we store a spare bag at the hotel while you trek. Pack light, keep your daypack organized, and everything else becomes easy.

Not sure which trek suits you? A short route like the Druk Path Trek is a good first step, and the Jomolhari Trek is a classic for those who want a bigger mountain. Tell us your fitness and your dates, and we will recommend the right one.
